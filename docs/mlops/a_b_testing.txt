Measurements:
   choose ones that transform into relevance for business goals.
   e.g. movie watch time, CTR
   - impressions (the top_k=20 shown to user)
     - count fractions by movie_tier
   - CTR
     - how often is a head, torso or tail clicked compared to the others?
       - if the tail items are never clicked, they weren't very relevant
         to the user.
   - coverage of unique movies across all users
     - count this in treatment vs control during the test window.
   - log the funnel from k=100 to k=20
     - retrieval by movie_tier as normalized fraction
       for control compared to treatment should show
       shift from head to toros and tail for the treatment group
     - ranker by movie_tier as normalized fraction
       - e.g. if no movie_tier=2 (niche) make it to top_k=20
         then the objective of diversity and personalization
         for niche movie watchers will have failed.

If one objective is to increase catalog discovery
then increase in tail impressions and catalog coverage
may be a worth while trade-off for small decrease in CTR,
but not if CTR for tail items is 0.
